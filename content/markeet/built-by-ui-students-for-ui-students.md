Markeet is not a company product.  
It is not backed by corporations, influencers, or external organisations.

Markeet was built by UI students who understand the everyday reality of campus life — the stress of finding affordable textbooks, the difficulty of selling items you no longer need, and the frustration of relying on scattered, distracting platforms that were never designed for students in the first place.

This platform exists because UI students identified a real problem and chose to solve it for their own community.

#### Why Supporting Student-Built Platforms Matters

Every strong campus ecosystem thrives on participation.  
  
When students support platforms built within their community, those platforms grow, improve, and become more valuable to everyone.

If UI students do not support solutions created by UI students, then who will?

Markeet depends on student engagement — not just in signing up, but in actively using the platform. Listing items, browsing, sharing the platform with friends, and giving feedback are all ways the community shapes what Markeet becomes.

The more students participate, the more useful Markeet becomes:

-   More listings mean better options for buyers
-   More buyers mean faster sales for sellers
-   More activity strengthens trust and reliability across the platform

This is how a student-driven marketplace grows.

#### A Platform Owned by the Community

Markeet is free to use and focused on one thing: connecting UI students to one another in a safe, distraction-free environment.

There are no middlemen.  
  
No paid promotions.  
  
No pressure to save contacts or spam people privately.

Just UI students helping UI students.

Because access is restricted to verified student email addresses, Markeet remains a trusted space where transactions happen between members of the same academic community, often at familiar locations around campus.

#### Your Role in Building Markeet

Markeet is still growing, and its future is shaped by the students who choose to use it.

You do not need to be selling something immediately to contribute. Simply joining, exploring listings, and sharing the platform with friends helps strengthen the network. If you have something to sell — a textbook, a phone, furniture, clothing, or any useful item — listing it helps someone else in the UI community.

This is a shared effort.

Every sign-up, every listing, and every recommendation helps move Markeet closer to its vision of becoming the largest and most reliable campus marketplace in Nigeria.

#### Let’s Build This Together

By choosing to use and promote platforms built within our community, we encourage innovation, collaboration, and solutions that truly reflect student needs.
