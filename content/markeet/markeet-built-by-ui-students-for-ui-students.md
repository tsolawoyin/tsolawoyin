Hello fellow UItes 👋🏽

My name is **Temidayo**, a Medicine and Surgery student at the **University of Ibadan**. Together with **Apera Veronica**, a Physiology student at UI, we are excited to introduce to you **Markeet**.

**Markeet** is an **installable web application** designed to connect **buyers and sellers within the University of Ibadan**—both regular and DLC students—on one focused, distraction-free platform.

#### The Problem

Connecting with people to buy or sell items within campus has always been difficult. Currently, students rely on a few common methods:

1.  **Influencers** – who may charge fees or require personal connections
2.  **Manual outreach** – which is stressful, time-consuming, and often discouraging
3.  **Social media platforms** (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, etc.) – which are not built specifically for UI students and are highly distracting

You often go online to sell or buy something, only to end up doing something else entirely—most likely laughing your time away.



#### The Solution: Markeet

Markeet was built to solve these problems—**simply and directly**.

With Markeet:

-   There is **no middleman**. You connect directly with buyers or sellers.
-   You can reach a **large audience of UI students** instantly.
-   No need to save unwanted contacts or spam people privately.
-   The platform is **focused and distraction-free**.

You can easily upload items such as:

-   Textbooks
-   Furniture
-   Clothes
-   Phones
-   Laptops
-   And much more

All **completely free of charge**.

#### Security & Trust

Your safety matters to us.

-   Only **verified UI students** are allowed on Markeet

-   Sign-up is done **strictly with student email addresses**

-   Transactions and meet-ups happen at **familiar and safe locations** like:

    -   SUB
    -   Love Garden
    -   KDL
    -   Lecture theatres
    -   Halls of residence

Markeet is **built by UI students, for UI students**.
